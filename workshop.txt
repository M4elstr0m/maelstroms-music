description=[h1]Maelstrom's Music[/h1]
description=[i]Because the apocalypse deserves a soundtrack.[/i]
description=
description=A Project Zomboid mod that lets you turn your own MP3, OGG, or WAV files into fully tunable radio stations, TV channels, an adaptive background soundtrack, or a custom main menu theme.
description=[b]No complex audio editing, no extra UIs, no sound-bank hacking: just drop audio files in your mod folder or install an addon.[/b] Designed to merge a lot of music related features in a single mod.

description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=One mod to rule them all. It should basically gather every music mods in one.
description=Without any addon installed, this mod only adds on his own a sample radio station with 4 free-of-use jazzy music.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Unlimited Radio & TV Stations:[/b] Add as many as you like! Frequency numbers are randomly spread out so large music libraries won't clump together on lower channels.
description=[*][b]Adaptive Background OST:[/b] Replaces vanilla background music with your own tracks mapped to the game's dynamic 0–10 "drama" scale (reacts live to zombie threats).
description=[*][b]Custom Main Menu Theme:[/b] Seamlessly carries your custom menu music through the loading screen into gameplay.
description=[*][b]Distance-Based Volume:[/b] Handhelds, car stereos, and stationary TVs naturally fade with distance using vanilla audio physics.
description=[*][b]Zero Engineering Needed (when using addons):[/b] Drop your audio files into a folder alongside a tiny JSON configuration file.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility & Save Games[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Save Games:[/b] Safe to add or remove mid-save.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer:[/b] Fully compatible (Build 42.20+). [i]Note: Track selection is client-side, so different players tuned into the same station may hear different songs at the same moment.[/i]
description=[*][b]Other Mods:[/b] Zero vanilla soundbank files are modified, minimizing mod conflicts.
